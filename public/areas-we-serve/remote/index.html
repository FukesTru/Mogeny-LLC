Working remotely

How does working remotely with Mogeny work?

  1. Introductory video call

    We talk through your finances, your software and what you need, by video call or phone.

  2. Proposal by email

    You receive the scope and the price in writing. Nothing starts until you agree.

  3. Shared access

    You give us access to your accounting software, and read-only access to your bank accounts wherever the bank offers it.

  4. Monthly rhythm

    We close the books, send the reports and walk through them with you on a video call.

What you need

What do you need to work with Mogeny remotely?

Very little. Most of it you probably have already.

  • A phone or a computer for video calls
  • Accounting software we can both use, or our help choosing one
  • A way to send receipts and documents, such as photos or a shared folder
  • A regular time each month to review the numbers together
Your state

Does it matter which state you are in?

Bookkeeping and financial reporting follow the same standards in every state. State and local taxes differ, so we keep your records ready for a tax preparer who knows your state.

Time zones

Which time zone do you work in?

Mogeny is in Chicago, on Central Time. We schedule calls at times that work for both of us, wherever you are in the US.

Questions

What do remote clients ask?

Can you work with a business in another state?

Yes. Mogeny works remotely with clients anywhere in the US.

Are all services available remotely?

Yes. Every service, from bookkeeping to forensic accounting, is available to remote clients.

Do we ever need to meet in person?

No. Remote clients can work with Mogeny entirely by video call, phone and email.

Can a family outside Illinois use family office services?

Yes. Family office and estate management are available remotely anywhere in the US, working alongside your local attorney and advisers.
