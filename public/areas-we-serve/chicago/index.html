Local requirements

How do Chicago and Illinois rules affect your books?

A Chicago business can owe taxes and filings to the City of Chicago, Cook County and the State of Illinois, as well as to the federal government.

Every Illinois LLC also files an annual report with the Illinois Secretary of State. Clean, current books make each of these easier for you and your tax preparer.

Mogeny keeps your records organized so the right numbers are ready when each deadline comes. The tax returns themselves stay with your tax preparer.
