Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains what personal information Mogeny LLC, a financial management firm in Chicago, IL, collects through this website, how we use it, how text messages work, and the choices you have. Questions about it can go to hello@mogeny.com or (773) 964-2446.

Effective October 21, 2026. This policy explains how Mogeny LLC, a financial management firm in Chicago, Illinois, handles personal information collected through www.mogeny.com.

Who is responsible for your information?

Mogeny LLC is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. You can reach us by email at hello@mogeny.com or by phone at (773) 964-2446.

What information do we collect?

We collect the information you choose to give us when you use the contact form or the chat, call us, send a text message or email us. This can include your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in and anything you write in your message.

The contact form and the chat on this website are provided by LeadConnector, the customer relationship system we use. What you type into the form or the chat goes to our account in that system so we can reply. LeadConnector may use cookies and similar technology to run the form and the chat.

When you visit the website, our hosting provider keeps standard server records, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the website secure and working. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address. This website does not use advertising cookies.

What should you not send us through the website?

Please never send passwords, bank or card account numbers, Social Security numbers or other sensitive financial details through the contact form, the chat, by text message or by regular email. If our work together needs that information, we will arrange a safer way to share it.

How do we use your information?

  • To reply to your enquiry and set up an introductory call
  • To prepare a proposal and provide the services you ask for
  • To keep business records that the law requires
  • To keep this website secure and working

Will we contact you by text message?

If you give us your mobile number through the contact form or the chat, or text us, we may send you text messages about your enquiry and about services you have asked for. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to stop text messages, or reply HELP for help. You can also ask us to stop by calling (773) 964-2446 or emailing hello@mogeny.com.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or your consent to receive text messages with anyone else for their marketing.

Do we sell or share your information?

No. We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the website and the business, such as our website host, our email provider and LeadConnector, and when the law requires it. Those providers may use it only to provide their service to us.

How do we handle client financial information?

Financial information you share with us as a client is used only to provide the services in your engagement and is shared only with the people you name, such as your tax preparer, attorney or investment adviser. The terms of your engagement with us also apply.

How long do we keep your information?

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to keep reasonable business records. We keep client records for as long as the engagement and the law require.

What choices do you have?

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Email hello@mogeny.com or call (773) 964-2446.

Do we collect information from children?

No. This website is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Do we collect biometric information?

No. Mogeny LLC does not collect biometric information, such as fingerprints or face scans, through this website.

Will this policy change?

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the effective date at the top of this page.
