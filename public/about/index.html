Who is Kamila Piotrowicz?

Kamila Piotrowicz owns and runs Mogeny LLC, a financial management firm based in Chicago, Illinois.

Through Mogeny, Kamila brings 6 services together in one firm. A client can move from catch-up bookkeeping to monthly reporting to CFO-level planning without changing providers, and a family can have its household, property and estate finances handled by the same firm.

Kamila works with business owners and families in Chicago in person, and with clients anywhere else in the US remotely.
